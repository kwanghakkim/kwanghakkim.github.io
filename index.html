Hi! I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at UC San Diego (UCSD), advised by Prof. Miroslav Krstić and Prof. Mamadou Diagne in the Nonlinear and Adaptive Control Laboratory. I work on safety-critical control of underactuated vehicles — systems such as cars, unicycles, and their aerial and marine counterparts that cannot move freely in every direction. Using control Lyapunov and barrier function (CLF/CBF) methods, I design feedback laws that keep these vehicles safe under tight limits on state, actuation, and uncertainty. My work includes nonholonomic vehicle parking, robust safety filters for unknown moving obstacles, and high-relative-degree safety constraints, with designs that can be safe, inverse-optimal, adaptive, or convergent in fixed or prescribed time. More recently, I have been extending this work into three dimensions, toward autonomous underwater and aerial vehicles (AUVs and UAVs).

I hold an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from UC San Diego and a B.S. in Aerospace Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin. Before my doctoral studies, I worked on autonomous systems as an undergraduate researcher under Prof. Ufuk Topcu.
